👋 What this is

Psychedelic Policy Reform is a policy and evidence initiative built on the Religious Freedom Restoration Act (RFRA), the federal law that limits how far the government may burden a person’s sincere exercise of religion. It’s seen from Pennsylvania, and it’s meant to serve communities whose sincere spiritual practice includes sacraments the law still restricts.

The work is plain: gather the evidence, explain the law in words anyone can follow, and keep an open record of every step. This AstroLog is that record. Its other half, the dApp preview, holds the evidence library’s shelves and a pledge of support you can try with a wallet signature (a preview: nothing is recorded yet).

Not legal advice. Everything here is general information about the law and public sources, written for understanding. It can’t account for anyone’s situation. For advice, talk to a lawyer licensed where you live.

🗂️ What’s in this repository

  • changelog/: one Markdown file per entry, named YYYY-MM-DD-short-slug.md.
  • library/: explainers and longer write-ups. A draft says so (draft: true), and anything about law or evidence carries the “not legal advice” note (legal: true).
  • src/, public/: the Astro site. public/arrival/ holds the arrival kit (v1.5.1) that both this AstroLog and the dApp preview load. Since kit 1.5 it welcomes visitors in English first, with Español one tap away; the kit’s own menus are translated and marked “under review”, and a quiet note says when a page’s words are still in English. public/brand/ holds this README’s banner.
  • ROADMAP.md and SUSTAINABILITY.md: where this is headed, and what keeps it running. Each is also a page on the site.

The source repository is private. On every push to main, one workflow builds the site and publishes only the finished pages to the gh-pages branch of psychedelic-policy-reform-astro-public, and another mirrors the allowlisted source (never changelog/, library/ or agent notes) to its main. The site also rebuilds every day, so the ledger stays fresh.

Open with a one-line TL;DR, then put the full story inside a <details> block. This is a policy and evidence lane: no one’s personal story or legal history ever goes here, and partners are named only once they’ve been announced.
