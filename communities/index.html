Christopher W, who started this initiative, also serves four life-changing healing communities, helping tend their homes on GitHub (Psanctuary has two). Each card below opens a community's GitHub home, where its own welcome greets you first, and each is described in its own public words.

Every one of those efforts helps, and this path walks alongside them, not in competition with them.

Looking ahead: a supporters' network of our own

Sneak peek

In time, supporters could sign a pledge with a wallet signature and gather in a small network that belongs to them rather than to one company.

  • Pledges signed with a wallet signature: no gas, and no personal details needed. The dApp preview lets you try one today, and nothing is recorded.
  • Tokens of gratitude, later: soulbound thank-yous that stay in the wallet they're given to, a deeper commitment beyond a signed pledge (a sneak peek on the pledge page).
  • Profiles and posts on open protocols like Lens and Farcaster, paired with a DAO where supporters propose ideas and decide together.
  • Privacy first: everyone chooses what's public.

No dates, and nothing to sign up for yet. This is a direction, not a promise.

The NORML groups are related, not partners: this initiative doesn't speak for them, and they don't speak for it. Please don't share personal legal details in any group; this initiative can't give legal advice.

Want to know when a space of our own opens? The changelog will say so first.
