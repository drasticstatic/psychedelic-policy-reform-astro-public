Reach out through the Facebook groups, too

TL;DR: The Reach out window now lists the two Facebook groups Christopher W manages, Pennsylvania Psychedelic Reform and Pennsylvania RFRA, as ways to reach the initiative, right beside GitHub. The two related NORML groups stay in the Community window, one tap away.
Read the full story

💬 What changed

On 10-22 the Facebook groups went into the Community window, where people gather. Christopher had asked for them in Reach out too: for a young initiative with no address or form yet, a group he manages is the most human way in. So the Reach out window now opens with:

  • Pennsylvania Psychedelic Reform, for anyone following psychedelic law and policy reform in Pennsylvania.
  • Pennsylvania RFRA, on religious-freedom law in Pennsylvania and what it means for sincere sacramental practice.

Then come GitHub and the two placeholders (a public address and a contact form), unchanged.

🤝 Why the NORML groups stay in Community

They’re related groups run by others. This initiative doesn’t speak for them, so it doesn’t list them as ways to reach it. The Reach out window links straight to the Community window, where all four groups are.

🌎 In Spanish too

The new words have Spanish versions alongside the rest of the site’s (AI-written and marked “translation in review” until a native speaker reads them).

Shared in Telegram, iMessage or Discord, it arrives with its own preview card.
